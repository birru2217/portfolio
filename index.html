About Me

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) student at Bule Hora University (BHU), currently holding a GPA of 3.8. Over the past few years, I've focused on building software that actually works well — not just code that runs, but systems that are clean, reliable, and easy to maintain.

I've worked across a few different areas: building an Enterprise Desktop System, developing responsive Web Platforms, and automating workflows using the Telegram API. Each project taught me something different, but the goal has always stayed the same — build something that solves a real problem, not just something that looks good on paper.

What actually drives me is seeing everyday problems around me and thinking, "this could be solved with a bit of code." That's where most of my ideas come from. I don't see technology as something separate from real life; I see it as a tool that can make ordinary processes faster, simpler, and less frustrating for the people who deal with them.

I also believe showing up consistently matters more than being naturally talented. I'm still learning, still improving, and I plan to keep building projects that push me to get better — one at a time.
